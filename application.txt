E-Commerce Application
 
Product Catalog
- Product listing is available.
- Product name and price are displayed.
- Product category is displayed.
- Product availability status is displayed.

Product Search
- Product can be searched by name.
- Search results are displayed.

Pricing
- Product prices can be updated.
- Updated prices are reflected in the product catalog.

Wishlisht
- Products can be added to a wishlisht.
- Wishlist items can be viewed separately.


Customer support
- Customer can view support information'
- Support request can be submitted.

Customer Notifications
- Customers can receive order status notifictions.
- Notification preferences can be managed by customers.
